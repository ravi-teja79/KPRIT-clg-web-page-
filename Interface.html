Welcome to Kommuri Pratap Reddy Institute of Technology, Ghanpur, Ghatkesar, Telangana.

Established in 2008, KPRIT provides engineering education in various branches.

About KPRIT

KPRIT was established in 2008 with a campus of about 22 acres.

The college provides classrooms, laboratories, library, sports, hostel and transportation facilities.

It is affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH).
