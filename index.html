About KPRIT

Kommuri Pratap Reddy Institute of Technology is also known as KPRIT. It was established in the year 2008.

The college has a campus of about 22 acres. It provides facilities such as classrooms, laboratories, library, sports facilities, hostel and transportation.

KPRIT is affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH).

The institute focuses on technical education, practical learning, innovation and the overall development of students.
